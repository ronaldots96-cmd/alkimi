Your 3 short videos

People book with a person, not with a logo. Each video is 20–30 seconds, shot vertical on the phone or with the camera guy. Talk to the camera like you're talking to one parent or one adult at the front desk. Use the scripts as a guide, not word for word. Your own phrasing is better.

Video 1 · "Come meet us"

For: ads to adults and the first follow-up message · Shot: you at the front door, academy sign visible

"Hi, I'm Chloe from Alkimi Jiu-Jitsu here in South Austin."

"If you've been thinking about trying a martial art, this is your sign. You don't need any experience. Most of our students started exactly where you are."

"Book a trial class and I'll be the one welcoming you at the door. See you on the mat."

Shot tips: daylight, phone at eye level, 2–3 takes, first word within the first second

Video 2 · "For parents"

For: ads to parents and the kids follow-up · Shot: on the mat, kids class warming up in the background

"Parents, does this sound familiar? Too much screen time, energy through the roof after school, and a hard time listening?"

"You're not alone. It's one of the most common things we hear. I'm Chloe from Alkimi Jiu-Jitsu, and that's exactly what our kids classes work on: they burn off energy, learn to focus and follow instructions, and build real confidence. No punches, no kicks."

"Bring your child for a trial class. You're welcome to stay and watch the whole thing."

Shot tips: film from the side of the mat. Kids in the background should be blurred or facing away unless parents have signed a consent form.

Video 3 · "The November offer"

For: the top of the Black Friday page and messages to cold leads · Shot: front desk, holding the starter kit

"This is the best offer we'll have all year."

"Start at Alkimi this November and get $30 off every month / the starter kit with your gi."

"Or, if the holidays make it crazy to start right now, lock in this price today with a $100 deposit and start in January."

"But hurry. This offer is only good through November. Tap the button and grab your spot."

Shot tips: open on the first line with energy, straight to camera. Shoot after Nicholas confirms the start-now option (highlighted).
03

Student testimonials

Short videos of real students and parents explaining why they joined. We have no testimonial videos yet, and these are what convinces people on the fence. Target: 6 videos of 30–45 seconds, 2 per week, all filmed by Nov 25.

Who to ask

  • 2 parents of kids aged 6 and up
  • 2 adults in Fundamentals who started from zero
  • 1 woman from the women's class
  • 1 student who started after 30

Pick people who are happy and comfortable on camera. Get a signed consent form from every adult, and from a parent for any child on camera.

What to ask (30–45 sec total)

  1. Why did you (or your kid) start?
  2. What's changed since you started?
  3. What would you tell someone who's on the fence?

Film vertical, in a quiet corner, with the mic on. One take per question is fine. We'll edit.

05b

How you help the automations work

We build and run the automations. You know the leads better than anyone, so these are the places where your input makes them sound like Alkimi and convert.

Material

  • The 5 questions leads ask you most, with your best answers. They become the "first class" guide and the FAQ.
  • The 3 most common reasons people say "not now" (price, schedule, timing…) and what you usually reply. We use your words in the follow-ups.
  • Read every message before it goes live. If it doesn't sound like you, change it. You're the one signing it.
  • Pick 10 photos you'd show a new parent or adult: mat, classes, coaches, kids.

Communication

  • Confirm the hours messages can go out and when you can answer replies.
  • When an automated message causes confusion or a complaint, tell us in the group the same day.
  • If a message ever interrupts a conversation you're having, flag it right away. That's the bug we most want to avoid.
  • Send a personal text to the 25 warmest leads (Follow Up To Close, Reach Out Later, No Show) before the automation starts.

Your daily order of priorities

  1. Replies from the automation (HOT LEADS, with a task assigned to you): answer within 1 hour.
  2. NEW LEADS: first reply within 5 minutes during open hours.
  3. Today's and tomorrow's trials: confirm, and rebook anyone in NO SHOW within 24 hours.
  4. FOLLOW UP TO CLOSE: anyone who did a trial and hasn't decided. Offer the January deposit if timing is the issue.
  5. REACH OUT LATER: only the ones whose date is today.

Daily check · 10 minutes

  • Every card you touched is in the right stage.
  • New members are marked Won. Anyone who said no is marked Lost with a reason.
  • Nothing is stuck: no trial date in the past still sitting in APPOINTMENT BOOKED.

Weekly check-in · bring 3 things

  • Which message got the most replies, and what people said.
  • The top reason people said no this week.
  • One thing that would make your week easier: a material, a message, a change to a step.
