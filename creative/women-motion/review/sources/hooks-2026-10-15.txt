	Visual & layout direction (all 9 ads)
* Background: dark charcoal/black, matching ALKIMI's existing palette — not a bright or generic gym-stock look. This keeps the premium, Valente-Brothers-style positioning intact.
* Hook placement: top third of the frame, bold, Poppins Bold (same family used on the pricing cards), white or brass, 8–10 words max so it reads in under two seconds.
* Promise placement: lower third, Montserrat, lighter weight, 1–2 lines.
* Photography: real students and coaches at the South Austin facility — never stock photography. Mid-action or mid-drill framing over posed shots, to keep it authentic.
* Logo: small ALKIMI wordmark in a bottom corner, consistent across all nine — same placement already used on the class-schedule graphics.
* Segmentation cue: a subtle accent color per campaign so the three audiences are distinguishable at a glance in Ads Manager, while staying inside the core charcoal/white/brass system — e.g. brass for men, a softer rose-gold for women, a cooler accent for kids.
* CTA: consistent per campaign, either baked into the image's bottom third or set as the ad's link-button text in Meta Ads Manager.
Next steps
* Confirm whether all 3 concepts per campaign run simultaneously as an A/B test, or launch one at a time per audience
