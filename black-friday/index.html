01What's included in the Black Friday offer?

You choose one of two options. Start this November and save almost $400 a year on your membership. Or pay a $100 deposit to lock in the same discount and start in January.

02How does the $100 deposit work?

It holds the Black Friday discount for you while you wait for January. The deposit is non-refundable. Begin training by January 31, 2027 to keep the discount. When you start, the full $100 is deducted from your membership dues. If you decide not to start, it is not returned.

03When does the offer end?

November 30, 2026, at 11:59 pm Central time. After that, regular pricing applies.

04How much is the membership?

With the Black Friday offer, the monthly membership is $235 for men, $225 for women and $215 for Toddlers (ages 3 to 5) and Kids (ages 6 to 14). These rates include the $30 monthly discount, which saves $360 over 12 months.

05I've never trained. Is that OK?

Yes. Fundamentals welcomes beginners from age 15. Toddlers is for ages 3 to 5, and Kids is for ages 6 to 14. No previous experience is needed.

06When are the classes?

Monday through Saturday, with early-morning, lunchtime and evening classes for adults, and afternoon classes for kids and toddlers. See the weekly schedule.

07Are the adult classes mixed?

Yes. In Fundamentals, men and women train together. We also have a women's class, 100% women and led by a female coach.

08What age can kids start?

From age 3. Toddlers (3 to 5) train in a semi-private class, and Kids (6 to 14) train in group classes split by age.

09What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable workout clothes are fine. A gi isn't required for your first class, and Alkimi can lend you one if needed.
