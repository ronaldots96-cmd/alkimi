This isn't about becoming physically stronger. It's about feeling safe in your own skin.

Real self-defense isn't about out-muscling anyone. It's about knowing what to do when it matters: how to keep your space, break a grip, get back up and get away. Jiu-Jitsu was built for exactly that, using leverage and technique instead of size. You start to carry yourself differently. Not a new body, but self-awareness, confidence and a kind of self-esteem nobody can take from you.

How the class works

Never done a martial art? Most of the women in our class hadn't either. Here's exactly how it works, so you never feel lost or out of place.

  • Women only, starting from zero. A class that's 100% women, of every age and background, and most of them started exactly where you are now.
  • You set the pace. No heavy sparring in your first weeks. One technique at a time, practiced with a partner under the coach's eye.
  • Real self-defense, not choreography. What you learn is built for real situations: staying calm under pressure, keeping your space, getting free and getting back up.
  • A community that has your back. Women who train together, cheer each other on and show up for each other, on and off the mat.

Taught by a woman, in a class just for women

Led by a female coach who knows what it takes to step onto the mat for the first time. No one watching or judging, just women of every age, many starting from zero. Class after class, you stand taller and trust yourself more. That's the transformation women tell us about.

Questions before your first class

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No, most people start from zero, with no physical preparation at all.

Will I be training with men?

No. The women's class is 100% women, led by a female coach.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes for your first class, we lend a gi if you decide to continue.

Is there an age limit?

No, we welcome everyone from young adults to more experienced women.

Is the trial class paid?

Yes. We charge a $20 commitment fee to hold your spot in class. If you decide to keep training, the $20 is credited toward your first month.
