Jiu-Jitsu works on what changes everyday life: the discipline to listen and follow through, respect for parents, elders and teammates, self-control when things get frustrating, and the self-confidence to stand tall, even in front of a bigger kid at school. How the class works

Never done martial arts? Most of our families hadn't either. Here's exactly how it works, so you and your child never feel lost.

  • Day one: the basics. We show your child how to put on the gi, tie the belt and how class runs, before any technique.
  • One hour, the same structure every class. Warmup and games, then one technique at a time, practiced with a partner under the coach's eye. Kids know what's coming, so it's easier to focus.
  • Three age groups. Ages 3 to 5, 6 to 9 and 10 to 15, so kids train with kids their own age and size and the coach can pay attention to each one.
  • Parents stay in the loop. The coach walks you through what to expect before class, and talks with you during and after class about how your child is doing. You're always welcome to watch.

Questions parents ask

What age can my child start?

From 3 years old. Classes are split into three age groups: 3 to 5, 6 to 9 and 10 to 15.

My child has never trained anything before, is that a problem?

No, most kids start from zero.

Does Jiu-Jitsu hurt?

There's no punching or kicking, so the risk is much lower than contact sports like football.

How long is each class?

Each class is 1 hour.

Is the trial class paid?

Yes. We charge a $20 commitment fee to hold your child's spot in class. If your child keeps training, the $20 is credited toward the first month.
