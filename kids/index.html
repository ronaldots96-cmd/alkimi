Bullying. Screen time. No boundaries. You've probably tried everything.

Most of the time, the problem isn't that your child isn't trying hard enough, it's that they don't have a system yet. Jiu-Jitsu teaches, in practice, what to do when things get out of control, whether it's a silly fall or facing a bigger kid at school. That's something you train. You can't improvise it.

How it actually works

  • Classes grouped by age and level, young kids never train with teenagers
  • Visible progress through belts and stripes, so your child can see their own growth
  • Jiu-Jitsu has no punches or kicks, the focus is on control and positioning, not impact
  • Small class groups with a coach's full attention the whole time, never just a crowd on the mat

Coaches who specialize in teaching kids

Every kids class at Alkimi is led by coaches who know how to work with children. They are patient, consistent and experienced in building confidence and discipline in young students, one class at a time. They know when to encourage, when to challenge, and how to keep every child engaged and safe on the mat. All of our coaches are hand-picked by Nicholas Meregali, world champion and one of the most respected names in Jiu-Jitsu.

What to expect at your child's first class

  1. Book and pay the $20 fee, this secures your child's spot
  2. Arrive 10 minutes early, no need for your own gi, we lend one
  3. Light warmup and floor games before any technique
  4. Coach introduces the group and explains the safety rules before starting
  5. First technique, always supervised, no heavy sparring for beginners

And if you'd like to stay and watch, you're always welcome. There's a seating area right next to the mats.
